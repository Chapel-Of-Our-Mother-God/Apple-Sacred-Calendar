Privacy Policy — Chapel Sacred Calendar

Chapel Sacred Calendar does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not require an account or sign-in.

The app uses the device's local date and time to calculate the sacred calendar date. App settings, such as the calendar panel position and notification preference, are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to us.

Some calendar entries contain links to pages on mother-god.com. These links open in the user's normal web browser. Any activity on that website is separate from the Chapel Sacred Calendar app and is subject to the website's own policies.

Chapel Sacred Calendar does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

For questions about this privacy policy, contact: chapelofourmothergod@proton.me
